Aim Humanization Backends

Dispatch (path_runner.cpp)

Switch on EAimControlMode per tick. Raw delta = angleDelta / (sens × m_yaw|m_pitch).

dx = pitchΔ / (sens·m_pitch)
dy = -yawΔ / (sens·m_yaw)
  • LegacySmooth — scalar EMA
  • AdaptiveServo — PID+jerk limit
  • WindMouse — stochastic+Fitts

Legacy Smooth

Fast & deterministic. Simple 1-pole. No humanization.

out = round(raw · (1−smooth))
  • Best for: recording/testing

Adaptive Servo (PID)

Per-axis cascade: v→a→j with cadence drift envelope.

a = clamp(resp·(v*−v), ±aMax)
v += a·dt (|j|<jMax)
  • Tight tracking · energy-shaped
  • Best for: near-lock follow

WindMouse + Fitts NEW

BenLand100 noise accumulator + Fitts's Law target velocity. Naturally slow near target.

mt = a + b·log₂(D/W+1)
v* = sign·(D/mt)
v += (v*−v)(1−e^−g·dt) + wind·dt
wind ← wind/√3 + rand·F/√5
  • Humanized start-glide-settle
  • Reuses HumanAxisState_t velocity/residual/acceleration

Extended Waypoint Format

Backwards-compatible with old paths.json: new fields are only written when non-default. Loader guards on is_number() / is_array(), flags are persisted as plain int.

pos / angleVector + QAngle (existing)
typeNormal · MultiBranch
branch_mode + next_indicesSequential · Random · Cycle
flags (bitmask)CROUCH · JUMP · WALK · SNIPER · LADDER · PRECISE
radiusPer-waypoint reach (<=0 → global)
wait_timeSeconds to pause on arrival
desired_speed0..1; <0.75 auto-holds Shift
gaze_targetReserved: aim override on arrival
runtime hooksJUMP pulses Space · WAIT gates motion

Runtime State Handoff

Arrival Handler

On currentWaypointIndex change: fire JUMP pulse, seed waitRemainingSeconds, rearm for next waypoint. Wait decrements dt and zeros motion until expired.

Crouch / Walk Resolution

WPF_CROUCH OR auto-crouch-on-lower-target. Walk = WPF_WALK OR desiredSpeed<0.75. Shift key released in all stop paths.

dt & Humanized Axes

ComputeControlDeltaSeconds seeds from first real sample (no more 1/120 hardcode). HumanAxisState_t resets on mode switch & stop.
